Professional Summary

I'm a PMP-certified IT project manager with over 17 years in the field. My first decade was hands-on: hosting support, Linux and VoIP servers, Microsoft infrastructure in Qatar, then designing cloud environments on AWS, Azure, GCP and DigitalOcean. Having done the technical work myself makes it easier to plan realistically and to spot a problem before it reaches the status report.

Since 2019 I've been at Royal Cyber, running projects and the global managed services support function for enterprise clients. The largest was a $15M SAP Commerce Cloud program for Fletcher Building across Australia and New Zealand. Others include AWS security and DevOps work for SUEZ in the US, and banking and government projects in Saudi Arabia.

Day to day, my job is keeping scope, budget and timelines honest, getting executives and engineers to agree on what "done" means, and putting SLA, KPI and service improvement routines in place so systems keep running well after go-live.
